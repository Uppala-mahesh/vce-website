01 · Vision

Our vision.

To be a premier engineering institution imparting quality technical education and inculcating human values, contributing to the socio-economic development of the nation and the world.

The vision is deliberately unambitious in the SaaS-vernacular sense — no talk of "disruption," no talk of "reimagining." It states plainly what a good engineering college is meant to do: teach engineering well, teach it to people who will act with integrity, and contribute to the country's economic and social base.

02 · Mission

Four pillars.

The mission is expressed as four operational commitments — each measurable, each with a live budget line, each reviewed by the Governing Body annually.

01

Quality Technical Education

Delivering rigorous, current, and industry-aligned curricula across all seven UG programmes and four PG schemes, with periodic revision by the Board of Studies and Academic Council.

02

Research & Innovation

Fostering a research culture through the R&D Cell, funded projects, patent filings, and doctoral supervision — building on the college's autonomous status.

03

Human Values & Ethics

Embedding professional ethics, environmental awareness, and community engagement through NSS, Street Cause, and mandated courses on the Constitution of India.

04

Industry & Alumni Engagement

Maintaining active partnerships with recruiting firms, research organisations, and an alumni network that spans four decades — sustaining the pipeline from campus to career.

03 · Programme Educational Objectives

What our graduates should be doing.

PEOs describe career and professional accomplishments expected of Vasavi graduates approximately five years after graduation. They are department-specific but converge on three central themes.

PEO 1
Professional Practice
Graduates will be employed in engineering, IT, research, or civil-service roles that require both technical competence and independent professional judgement.
PEO 2
Higher Learning & Research
Graduates will pursue advanced study — master's and doctoral programmes in engineering, applied sciences, or management — or engage in industrial research and development.
PEO 3
Leadership & Ethics
Graduates will demonstrate leadership, ethical practice, and social responsibility in their profession — advancing both their organisations and the communities they serve.
