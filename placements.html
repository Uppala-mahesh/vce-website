03 · Placement Process

How the cycle runs.

The T&P Office runs a structured recruitment cycle from July through April. Companies register in advance, get shortlists based on eligibility criteria, and conduct on-campus or hybrid rounds.

Jul
Registration
T&P registers eligible students · profiles ready
Aug
Recruiter Outreach
Company invitations · JD collection · slot booking
Sep–Oct
Pre-Placement Talks
Company presentations · Q&A · shortlists
Nov–Feb
Interviews
Written / online tests · technical + HR rounds
Feb–Mar
Offers
Offer letters issued · student acceptance
Apr
Cycle Closes
Final placement data compiled · reports published
04 · Preparation Programme

Placement readiness begins in Semester V.

The T&P Office runs a structured preparation programme through Semesters V, VI, and VII — aptitude tests, mock interviews, coding contests, and one-on-one profile reviews.

Sem V
Foundations
Aptitude workshops · Introduction to placement tests · Resume writing · Company profiling.
Sem VI
Skill Building
Coding contests (weekly) · Group discussions · Mock technical interviews · Domain deep-dives.
Sem VII
Placement-Ready
Full mock recruitment cycles · Company-specific prep · One-on-one interview coaching.
Sem VIII
Off-Campus Support
Post-cycle support for students not placed on-campus · alumni referrals · off-campus openings.
05 · Internships

Mandatory summer internship.

Under R-24, every B.E. student must complete a summer internship between Semester VI and VII — minimum 6 weeks, ideally 8. Placements Office maintains a company database and helps place students.

Duration
6–8 weeks · Summer between Sem VI & VII
Credits
2 credits under R-24 curriculum
Evaluation
Company certificate + on-campus presentation
Support
Company database · introduction letters · stipend negotiation

VCE has an active alumni network with over 15,000 graduates across four decades. The alumni association runs referrals, mentorship programmes, and industry-visit hosting for current students.

07 · Invite VCE for Recruitment

For recruiters.

Companies interested in recruiting from VCE can register through the T&P Office. We support standard on-campus, hybrid (online tests + on-campus interviews), and fully-remote recruitment models.

Get in touch

Email tpo@vce.ac.in or call +91 40 2314 6079 (extn. 108). The T&P team responds within 48 hours during the academic year.
