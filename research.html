01 · R&D Cell

A coordinating body, not a bureaucracy.

The R&D Cell was established in 2015 to formalise what departments had already been doing informally: pursuing funded research, filing patents, publishing papers, and mentoring doctoral scholars. The Cell's job is to reduce paperwork friction and connect faculty to funding agencies — not to gatekeep.

Chaired by the Dean (Research), with departmental research-coordinators as members. Meets quarterly. Publishes an annual research bulletin.
