01 · Odd Semester

Semester I · III · V · VII.

The odd semester runs from mid-July through mid-December. Includes two CIE cycles, mid-semester break in October, and end-semester examinations in November–December.

02 · Even Semester

Semester II · IV · VI · VIII.

The even semester runs from early January through early June. Includes two CIE cycles, cultural fête in March, project-review windows for final-year students, and end-semester examinations in May.
