§ Campus Life/Events · Clubs · Societies · Athletics

Beyond the syllabus.

Three flagship events anchor the calendar. A long tail of societies, cells, and clubs fills the rest. Vasavi's campus life is built around the idea that engineering education is undermined, not enhanced, by an all-work culture.

§01 · Flagship Events

Three, every year.

Est. 1998 · 27th Edition · September 2026

Acumen 2026.

Technical Symposium · Three Days

The college's flagship technical symposium — paper presentations, hackathons, coding contests, robotics competitions, and departmental technical events across all seven engineering disciplines.

Draws over 3,000 participants from engineering colleges across Telangana, Andhra Pradesh, and Karnataka. Corporate sponsors provide prize pools and internship offers to winning teams.

Est. 1985 · 40th Edition · February 2026

Euphoria 2026.

Cultural Fête · Three Days

The college's oldest continuously-run event — a three-day cultural festival featuring music, dance, theatre, literary competitions, fine arts, and the traditional-day parade that has become the emotional signature of every graduating batch.

Celebrity headliners in past editions have included A.R. Rahman's band, Karsh Kale, and MTV Unplugged sessions. The event operates on a fully student-run committee under a Vice-Principal's oversight.

Kriti 2026.

Annual Art Exhibition · One Week

A curated exhibition of student work spanning architecture models, photo essays, engineering installations, painting, sculpture, and multimedia. Kriti is the youngest of the three flagships and has quietly become the college's most critically-praised event.

Previous editions have featured collaborations with local artists' collectives (Kalakriti, Muse Art Gallery) and been reviewed in The Hindu, Deccan Chronicle, and design publications.

§02 · Clubs, Cells & Societies

Twenty-plus student bodies.

Club-01 · Est. 1985
NSS · National Service Scheme
Community engagement, blood-donation drives, rural outreach programmes. Two-year mandatory-elective for interested students.
Club-02 · Est. 1998
Street Cause · VCE Chapter
Registered non-profit, entirely student-run. Runs education programmes in nearby government schools.
Club-03 · Est. 2015
Swayam · Entrepreneurship Cell
Startup incubation, pitch competitions, mentor connections. Portfolio includes three funded student ventures.
Club-04 · Est. 2019
Gender Equity Cell
Workshops on gender sensitisation, safety audits, mentorship programmes for women in engineering.
Club-05 · Est. 1988
IEEE Student Branch
Chapter of the international IEEE. Runs technical workshops, paper contests, and student-branch conferences.
Club-06 · Est. 1992
CSI Student Chapter
Computer Society of India chapter. Coding contests, industry visits, tech talks. Strongest in CSE / IT.
Club-07 · Est. 2003
SAE Baja Team
Undergraduate off-road vehicle team. Design, build, test, and race an all-terrain vehicle at the SAE Baja India event.
Club-08 · Est. 2010
SAE Supra Team
Formula-style single-seater race car project. Full CAD-through-fabrication annual build cycle.
Club-09 · Est. 2005
Robotics Club
Autonomous robot design and competition. Participates in Techkriti, ROBOCON, and inter-college contests.
Club-10 · Est. 2001
Traditional Day Committee
The single most anticipated day on the VCE calendar. Elaborate saree-and-kurta procession, folk performances, and inter-year competitions.
Club-11 · Est. 2011
Photography Club
Weekly photo walks, exhibition curation, event coverage. Annual publication in Kriti.
Club-12 · Est. 1996
Debate Society
British Parliamentary and Asian Parliamentary format debating. Regional circuit participation.
Cell-01 · Est. 2009
Anti-Ragging Cell
Statutory cell under UGC regulations. 24/7 helpline, complaint mechanism, monthly reporting.
Cell-02 · Est. 2016
Innovation & Startup Cell
Runs the annual startup expo, hosts Y-Combinator startup school locally.
Club-13 · Est. 2018
Music Club · Rhythms
Instrumental and vocal group. Annual concert during Euphoria; guest performances at civic events.
