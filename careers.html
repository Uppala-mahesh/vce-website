02 · Eligibility

AICTE norms.

Faculty positions follow AICTE eligibility norms and Osmania University recruitment procedures. Assistant Professors need Ph.D. (or M.E./M.Tech. with NET/GATE). Associate and Full Professor roles require Ph.D. + specified teaching/research experience.

Professor
Ph.D. + 10 yrs teaching/research · publications · doctoral supervision
Associate Professor
Ph.D. + 8 yrs · publications required
Assistant Professor
M.E./M.Tech. with NET/GATE · Ph.D. preferred
Staff Positions
Position-specific · check individual JD
03 · Selection Process

Application → shortlist → interview.

  1. Submit online application with CV, publication list, and cover statement
  2. Shortlist by department Selection Committee
  3. Written subject test (assistant / associate positions)
  4. Teaching demonstration + interview by Selection Committee
  5. Recommendation forwarded to Governing Body for approval
  6. Offer letter issued · joining typically within 30 days

06 · How to Apply

One portal. One CV. One deadline.

All applications submitted through the online recruitment portal. Only PDF uploads accepted. Applications by email are not processed. Late submissions are not entertained.

Deadline

30 September 2025 · 5 PM IST. Interview call letters sent by 10 October. Interviews conducted between 15 October and 15 November at the VCE campus.
