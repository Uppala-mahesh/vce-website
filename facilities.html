01 · Central Library

40,000 volumes. 180 seats.

Facility-01 · Library Block · Ground & First Floor

A working library.

The Central Library is open from 8 AM to 9 PM on all working days and 9 AM to 5 PM on Saturdays. It carries a print collection of over 40,000 volumes, subscribes to 200+ current journals, and provides 24×7 access to IEEE, ACM, Springer, and Elsevier online databases.

Two blocks. 600 residents.

Two hostel blocks — Boys' Hostel (400 capacity) and Girls' Hostel (200 capacity) — both on-campus, both with attached mess. Priority is given to out-of-station and international students.

03 · Sports & Gym

Outdoor grounds and an indoor complex.

The sports facility includes a full outdoor cricket ground with practice nets, football / hockey ground, six tennis courts, four basketball courts, two volleyball courts, an indoor badminton hall, and a gymnasium with cardio + strength equipment.

Outdoor
Cricket · Football · Hockey
Full-size grounds with practice nets and floodlights for evening play.
Court Sports
Tennis · Basketball · Volleyball
6 tennis · 4 basketball · 2 volleyball courts. Coach-supervised evening slots.
Indoor
Badminton · Table Tennis · Gym
Indoor hall with 8 badminton courts, 4 TT tables. Gym with cardio + strength.

The college competes annually in the Osmania University inter-collegiate championships and has a longstanding record in cricket, badminton, and athletics.

04 · Transport

TSRTC integrated.

Two Vasavi-dedicated TSRTC bus routes (5K and 66G) connect the campus to Hyderabad Deccan, Secunderabad, Kukatpally, Uppal, and LB Nagar. Monthly bus passes are available at ₹1,500. Private auto-rickshaw and cab access via campus main gate.

Route 5K
Secunderabad → Ameerpet → Mehdipatnam → Ibrahimbagh (VCE)
Route 66G
Golconda → Ibrahimbagh (VCE) → Tolichowki → Kukatpally
Frequency
Every 20 minutes during college hours · reduced service after 6 PM
Metro (upcoming)
Nearest station: Golconda (Corridor III extension · under construction · expected 2027)
05 · Medical Unit

On-campus infirmary.

The medical unit runs on all working days from 9 AM to 5 PM with a full-time nurse on duty. A consulting physician visits the campus twice a week (Wednesday and Saturday afternoons). Ambulance access via campus main gate; nearest tertiary hospital is the Nizam's Institute of Medical Sciences, 8 km east.

Emergency

Campus security control room · 24×7 · maintains emergency contact numbers for the nearest four hospitals. Dial extension 100 from any campus phone.

07 · Wi-Fi & IT

Campus-wide, 24×7.

Enterprise Wi-Fi coverage across all academic blocks, the library, both hostels, and outdoor commons. Authentication via institutional Office 365 credentials. Uplink: dual-fibre from BSNL and Airtel with automatic failover.
