01 · B.E. Admissions

Undergraduate — via TS EAMCET.

Every B.E. seat at Vasavi is allocated through the Telangana State Engineering, Agriculture, and Medical Common Entrance Test (TS EAMCET) counselling. There is no separate VCE entrance test, no management-quota-only intake outside the regulated 30% NRI/Category-B window.

Appear for TS EAMCET
Register and appear for the state entrance exam. Exam typically conducted in May; results announced in June.
Conducted by · JNTUH
Register for Counselling
Once EAMCET results are out, register with TSCHE (Telangana State Council of Higher Education) for engineering counselling.
Portal · tsche.ac.in
Certificate Verification
Original certificate verification at a designated Help-Line Centre. Vasavi hosts a Help-Line Centre — students may verify here directly.
Duration · 30 min
Web Options / Preferences
Fill web options — order colleges and branches by preference. Options can be revised until the freeze window closes.
Multiple rounds available
Seat Allotment
Seats allotted based on rank, preferences, and category. If allotted VCE, download allotment order and pay the tuition fee.
Rounds · 3 typical
Reporting at VCE
Report at the college with allotment order, fee receipt, and original certificates. Complete admission formalities at the Admissions Office.
Building · Admin Block
Category-B (Management & NRI Quota)

30% of seats are reserved as Category-B — allocated through the college on the basis of academic merit. Notification issued in June-July each year; separate application process. Details are published on this page during the intake cycle.

02 · M.E. / M.Tech. Admissions

Postgraduate engineering — via GATE.

Postgraduate engineering admission is through the Graduate Aptitude Test in Engineering (GATE) followed by state PGECET counselling. Non-GATE candidates admitted only when GATE-eligible seats remain unfilled after all rounds.

Eligibility
B.E. / B.Tech. in relevant branch · minimum 50%
Entrance
GATE — Graduate Aptitude Test in Engineering
Counselling
TS PGECET · TSCHE
Duration
2 years (4 semesters)
03 · MBA Admissions

Two-year MBA — via TS ICET.

The MBA programme under the School of Management Studies is a two-year AICTE-approved MBA. Admission is through the Telangana State Integrated Common Entrance Test (TS ICET) followed by TSCHE counselling.

Eligibility
Any bachelor's degree · minimum 50%
Entrance
TS ICET
Duration
2 years (4 semesters)
Seats
60 (AICTE approved)

08 · Frequently Asked

Common questions.

Q
Is there a direct admission process?
No. All Category-A seats go through TS EAMCET counselling. Category-B seats are allocated on academic merit by the college.
Q
What's the cut-off rank?
Varies by branch and year. Historical closing ranks are published post-counselling. CSE and IT close earliest (typically within 5000 rank).
Q
Is hostel accommodation guaranteed?
Hostel seats are limited and allocated on distance-from-hometown priority. Out-of-station students given first preference.
Q
Can I change branches?
Branch change is permitted only at the end of the first year, based on CGPA and vacancy. Governed by the R-24 regulation.
