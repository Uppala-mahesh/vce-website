03 · UGC Status

Section 2(f) & 12(B).

VCE is recognised under UGC's Section 2(f) — which formally establishes it as a college fit to receive assistance — and Section 12(B) — which makes the college eligible for central grants.

04 · Autonomy Order

Ten-year autonomous status.

UGC first granted autonomous status to VCE for the period 2014–15 to 2019–20. That was subsequently extended for a ten-year block, 2021–22 through 2030–31.

Initial Grant
2014–15 to 2019–20 (6 years)
Current Extension
2021–22 to 2030–31 (10 years)
UGC Order
Dated 15 April 2021
Affiliating University
Osmania University, Hyderabad
What this means, in practice

The college sets its own curriculum, conducts its own examinations, and issues its own results. Osmania University grants the degree; academic authority sits with VCE's own Academic Council.
