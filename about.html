01 · The Institution

Forty-four years, one motto.

Vasavi College of Engineering was established in 1981 by the Vasavi Academy of Education, a public charitable trust set up by prominent members of the Arya Vysya community of Hyderabad, with the intent to build a full-scale engineering institution serving the twin cities.

The college has grown from four founding departments in 1981 (Civil, Mechanical, Electrical, Electronics & Communication) to seven undergraduate programmes with a total sanctioned intake of 780, and four postgraduate schemes with an intake of 48. Every UG programme is currently accredited by the National Board of Accreditation.

The institution has been continuously affiliated to Osmania University since its founding, and holds autonomous status granted by the University Grants Commission — first for six years (2014–15 to 2019–20), and subsequently extended for a ten-year block from 2021–22 to 2030–31.

02 · Our History

A chronology.

Vasavi's institutional history is the story of steady expansion — not dramatic transformation. The college has added programmes, added labs, added accreditations, and added seats, at a measured pace over four decades.

1981
Founded
Vasavi College of Engineering is established by the Vasavi Academy of Education with four undergraduate departments: Civil, Mechanical, Electrical, and Electronics & Communication.
1988
Computer Science & Engineering
CSE department added — reflecting the emerging demand for software-trained engineers as the Indian IT industry took shape.
2001
Information Technology
IT department established as a distinct programme from CSE, with an applied focus on web systems, networks, and infrastructure.
2008
School of Management Studies
MBA programme launched, drawing on the college's already-established placement network and industry partnerships.
2014
Autonomous Status Granted
UGC grants autonomous status for a six-year period (2014–15 to 2019–20). Curriculum, examinations, and academic governance move in-house.
2019
SWAYAM-NPTEL Recognition
Recognised as "Best New Local Chapter" and rated "AA" for the July–December 2019 cycle.
2020
CSE (AI & ML)
New 60-seat programme launched under the CSE department to formalise a specialisation in data-forward roles.
2021
Autonomy Extended to 2031
UGC extends autonomous status for a ten-year period, from 2021–22 through 2030–31.
2022
NAAC A++ Grade
Reassessed by NAAC and awarded the A++ grade — the highest possible institutional grade under the 2022 methodology.
2025
44th Year
Current operating year. NBA re-accreditation cycle completed for all UG programmes. Placement rate: 92%.
03 · Vasavi Academy of Education

The founding body.

The Vasavi Academy of Education is a public charitable trust, formed in 1980, that owns and administers Vasavi College of Engineering. The Academy is non-profit and runs on grants, tuition-in-aid, and philanthropic endowment.

The Academy's governing board draws members from senior industry leaders, retired civil servants, chartered accountants, and legal counsel. Board terms are three years; the chairperson is elected from among the board members and cannot serve more than two consecutive terms.

Beyond the engineering college, the Academy also administers a junior college and a school in Hyderabad, though those operate as separate financial and academic units.

04 · Autonomous Status

What autonomy means, here.

Under the UGC's autonomous colleges scheme, VCE conducts its own admissions counselling supplement, sets its own curriculum, conducts its own examinations, and issues its own results — while remaining affiliated to Osmania University, which grants the degrees.

In practice this means: our Academic Council (not the university's) approves changes to the syllabus. Our Board of Studies (not the university's) decides on new electives. Our Controller of Examinations (not the university's) sets and grades papers. Osmania University's role is limited to degree conferral and quality oversight.

The current autonomy extension runs from 2021–22 through 2030–31, granted by the UGC in an order dated 15 April 2021.

Regulations in force

R-24 — applies to intake 2024 onwards. R-20 — applies to 2020–2023 intakes, running until each batch graduates.

05 · Location & Campus

Ibrahimbagh, Hyderabad.

The campus is a 6.5-acre site in Ibrahimbagh, west Hyderabad — approximately 12 km from Hyderabad Deccan railway station and 30 km from Rajiv Gandhi International Airport.

The site is bounded by the Nehru Zoological Park to the south and the residential neighbourhoods of Golconda and Langar Houz to the west. Public bus access is via TSRTC routes 5K and 66G; the nearest Metro station (currently under construction on the Corridor III extension) is Golconda.
