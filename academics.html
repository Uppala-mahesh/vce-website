02 · Regulations

R-24 replaces R-20.

A regulation is the operating agreement between the college and a student cohort — governing credits, evaluation, promotion, and graduation. Under autonomy, VCE issues its own. Two regulations are currently in force.

Current
R-24
Applies to 2024 intake onwards
  • 160 credits over 8 semesters (B.E.)
  • Continuous Internal Evaluation + Semester End Exam split 40/60
  • Choice-based Credit System (CBCS) with open electives
  • Mandatory Value-Added Course requirement (min. 4 credits)
  • Internship compulsory between Sem VI & VII (2 credits)
  • Capstone project — Sem VII & VIII (10 credits)
  • Minimum attendance: 75% per subject
Legacy
R-20
2020–2023 intakes · until they graduate
  • 160 credits over 8 semesters (B.E.)
  • CIE + SEE split 30/70
  • CBCS framework introduced
  • Value-added courses optional
  • Internship optional (elective)
  • Capstone project — Sem VIII only (8 credits)
  • Minimum attendance: 75% per subject

05 · Open Electives

Cross-departmental electives.

Open Electives are subjects offered by one department that students of other departments can register for. They broaden the graduate profile — a CSE student can take a course in Structural Design; a Mechanical student can take Machine Learning Foundations.

Open Electives are offered in Semesters V, VI, and VII under R-24. Total credit requirement across the programme: 9 credits.
