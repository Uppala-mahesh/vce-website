Fifteen thousand graduates.

The Vasavi Alumni Association was formalised in 1992 and now maintains an active roster of over 15,000 members across four decades of graduating classes. The Association runs referrals, mentorship, industry-visit hosting, and an annual homecoming during Euphoria.

01 · Vasavi Alumni Association

Registered · Autonomous · Volunteer-run.

The Association is a registered non-profit under the Telangana Societies Registration Act. It is funded entirely by member subscriptions and voluntary contributions. Membership is lifetime; there is no annual fee.

Founded
1992 · Silver Jubilee marked 2017
Membership
Lifetime · one-time contribution ₹1,000
Members
~15,200 active as of 2025
President
Sri Ramesh Rao · CSE 1993 · CEO, a Hyderabad-based product startup
Secretary
Ms. Anjana Kaul · ECE 2001 · Principal Engineer, an MNC
Join

Every VCE graduate is eligible for lifetime membership on payment of a one-time ₹1,000 contribution. Register at alumni@vce.ac.in with your batch, department, and current role.

03 · Mentorship Programme

One-on-one, one-year.

The Alumni Mentorship Programme pairs current final-year students with alumni mentors for a one-year engagement. Roughly 200 pairs each year; focus on placement preparation, career direction, and industry insight.

Format
1:1 Pairing
One alum · one student · one-year engagement.
Cadence
Monthly Sync
Minimum one hour per month · in-person or remote.
Signup
June Intake
Registration opens for Sem VII students each June.
04 · Giving Back

Endowments and scholarships.

The Alumni Association runs three institutional funds — the Merit Scholarship Endowment, the Financial Aid Fund, and the Lab Modernisation Fund — all funded entirely by alumni contributions.

Merit Endowment
Corpus ₹2.4 crore · funds top-rank scholarships
Financial Aid
Corpus ₹1.6 crore · need-based aid for underprivileged students
Lab Modernisation
Corpus ₹3.2 crore · departmental lab equipment grants
Contribute
Online via alumni.vce.ac.in/give · tax-deductible under 80G
