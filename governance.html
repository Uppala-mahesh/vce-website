Who runs the institution.

Governance at VCE is not centralised in a single office. Academic decisions sit with the Academic Council. Institutional finances sit with the Governing Body. Curriculum sits with departmental Boards of Studies. This page maps that structure.

03 · Vasavi Academy of Education

The founding trust.

The Academy is a registered public charitable trust, formed in 1980. It owns the campus and appoints the Governing Body of the college. The Academy trustees serve three-year terms with a maximum of two consecutive re-appointments.

Beyond VCE, the Academy administers Vasavi Junior College and Vasavi High School — though those operate as separate financial and academic units under the same trust umbrella.

04 · Academic Council

The academic authority.

Under UGC autonomy, the Academic Council is the highest academic body at VCE — approving syllabi, examination policy, and evaluation schemes. It is chaired by the Principal and includes all Heads of Department, subject experts, and university nominees.

Recent Council Decisions

Adoption of R-24 regulation for 2024 intake · Introduction of Value-Added Courses framework · PO/PSO attainment measurement standardised across all programmes.

05 · Board of Studies

Department-level curriculum boards.

Each department maintains its own Board of Studies — comprising department faculty, external subject experts, industry representatives, and alumni. BoS recommendations go to the Academic Council for approval. Meetings twice per academic year.

The BoS is the practical mechanism by which VCE keeps its curriculum current — new subjects, retired subjects, elective additions, and industry-driven revisions all originate here.
